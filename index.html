Rules

Be respectful

Treat all members with kindness and respect. Harassment, hate speech, personal attacks and discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated. This server is international, so the only language allowed is English.

No spamming or self-promotion

Avoid flooding channels with rapid text, repetitive emojis or unauthorized links. Self-promotion and referral links are only allowed in designated channels.

Keep content appropriate

Keep all discussions and media appropriate for the channel. Explicit, graphic or illegal content is strictly prohibited outside of designated NSFW spaces.

Use channels correctly

Post content in its relevant channel. For example, keep clips in the clips channel and game discussion in the chat.

No unsolicited DMs

Respect other members’ privacy. Do not DM members with spam, unwanted advertisements or inappropriate messages.

Follow Discord’s Terms of Service

All members must follow Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Listen to staff

Moderators and admins have the final say. If a staff member asks you to stop or move a conversation, please comply.
